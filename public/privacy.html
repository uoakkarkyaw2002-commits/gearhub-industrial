1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how SkyShopMM (Android package com.skyshopmm.app, website skyshopmm.com) collects, uses, shares and protects your information. SkyShopMM is developed and operated by SkyShopMM in Myanmar and is published on Google Play by TECH4MM COMPANY LIMITED. In parts of the app the platform is also called GearHub. SkyShopMM lets shops open an online store and lets buyers browse, chat with shops and place orders.

2. Information we collect

  • Google account sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture from Google when you choose “Continue with Google”. We never see your Google password.
  • Shop information (shop owners): shop name, logo, description, contact phone and address, products, prices, stock, photos, Excel/CSV files you upload, payment account details you choose to show buyers (for example KBZPay, WavePay or bank account), and optional profile details such as birthday and work.
  • Orders (buyers): the name, phone number and delivery address you enter, the items you order and the payment receipt picture you upload.
  • Messages and social activity: chat messages, stickers and voice messages you send to a shop or buyer; likes, comments, follows, posts and saved posts.
  • Voice calls: calls connect your phone directly to the other person. The sound of the call is not recorded or stored by us; we only keep a line in the chat such as “Voice call · 1:23” or “Missed voice call”.
  • Camera and photos: only when you use the photo scanner or upload photos. Scanner pictures are used to find matching products.
  • Microphone: only when you record a voice message or make or answer a voice call.
  • Notifications: if you turn notifications on, your device's push subscription address so we can send you order, chat and call alerts.
  • AI Assistant: the assistant works on your device. If you are signed in, your conversation with it, your to-do notes and which alerts you have seen are saved to your account so they are not lost.
  • Technical data: a login cookie to keep you signed in, settings saved on your device, and basic logs (such as time, page and error) that our server keeps to run and protect the service. We measure connection speed in the app only to show it to you.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS or call history. SkyShopMM does not show advertising.

3. How we use information

  • To run the app: sign you in, show shops and products, deliver chat messages and calls, and process orders between buyers and shops.
  • To send alerts you asked for (new orders, messages, calls).
  • To keep the service safe, prevent abuse and fix problems.
  • To keep backups so data is not lost.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share information with

  • Shops and buyers: when you order, the shop receives your order details (name, phone, address, receipt). When you chat, the other person sees your name and messages. Public shop pages, products, posts and comments can be seen by other users.
  • Service providers who host or process data for us only to run SkyShopMM: Render (server hosting), Cloudflare R2 (file and backup storage), Google (sign-in and Google Drive backups) and the push services of Google, Apple and browser makers (notification delivery). Calls may pass through Google STUN servers to connect.
  • Law: when required by law or to protect users and the service.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account and shop data while your account is active. When you delete your account, your account, shop, products, photos, files, messages and notification subscriptions are deleted within 30 days. Order records may be kept up to 90 days for accounting, then deleted. Backup copies are removed as backups rotate.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your shop, products and profile in the app.
  • You can turn notifications off in Settings → Notifications or in your phone settings, and deny camera or microphone access at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy, correction or deletion of your data. See Delete your account or contact us.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Access to each shop's data is limited to that shop's owner and the platform owner, and passwords are never stored because sign-in uses Google. No method is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Children

SkyShopMM is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will be shown in the app.
